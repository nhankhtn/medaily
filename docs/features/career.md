# Career

Career is three cards: **Skills**, with where each one is and where you want it; **Achievements**, captured when they happen rather than remembered at review time; and **Portfolio**, the work you can point to.

| | |
| --- | --- |
| **Where** | `/career` · sidebar → Life · phone → **More** |
| **Works offline** | No |
| **Needs** | Nothing extra |

## Rules

- A skill's level is from 1 to 5; its target is optional.
- An achievement's date is today or earlier.
- Portfolio tech is comma-separated.
- Entries are added, not edited: the page has no way to change or remove one yet.

## Add a skill

1. Open **Career** and press **Add skill**.
2. Enter a name, a category, a level from 1 to 5, and optionally a target level.
3. Save. The skill is drawn as five pips — current level filled, target outlined — with how far there is still to go.

## Record an achievement

1. Press **Add achievement** in the page header.
2. Enter what happened, the date (today or earlier), the impact and a link.
3. Save. The list shows each achievement with its date and impact.

## Add a portfolio item

1. On the **Portfolio** card, press **Add item**.
2. Enter a title, a link, a description and the tech involved, comma-separated.
3. Save.

## How it works

**Capture when it happens.** An achievement written down the day it happens keeps its impact and link; one remembered at review time has lost both.

**Pips show the gap.** Drawing the current level filled and the target outlined makes the distance still to go visible at a glance.

## Limits

- No entry can be changed or removed from the page yet — skills, achievements and portfolio items are add-only.
